# 3D Model Browser

A Windows app that turns a folder of 3D-printing models (STL, 3MF and OBJ) into a library you can see and search.

**[Download 3D Model Browser.exe](https://github.com/rendez2k/3d-model-browser/raw/main/3D%20Model%20Browser.exe)**

## Getting started

1. Download the file above and put it wherever you like (the Desktop is fine).
2. Open it, and choose the folder your models are in.

It needs Windows 10 or 11 and nothing else: there's nothing to install. The first time you open it, Windows may
say it doesn't recognise the app. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

After that it keeps itself up to date: when there's a newer version it fetches it quietly, and you have it the
next time you open the app. You can turn that off in Settings.

## What it does

- Shows one card per model, with a colour 3D preview, and lets you turn any model round in 3D.
- Search, favourites, "printed" ticks, tags, notes and categories.
- A "Print Next" list for what you want to print soon.
- Each model's size in millimetres, with a warning if it's too big for your printer.
- Print time and filament for projects you've already sliced in Bambu Studio or Orca.
- Opens models in Bambu Studio or Snapmaker Orca, or drag a model straight into your slicer.
- Unpacks downloaded .zip, .7z and .rar files and tidies their names.
- Can keep a copy of your library on Google Drive.

This page only holds the finished app. `latest.json` is the small file the app reads to see whether it's out of date.
